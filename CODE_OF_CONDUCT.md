# Code of Conduct

Formhand is used by people during a stressful time (job hunting). Be kind here.

## Our standard
- Be respectful and patient, especially with newcomers and with people describing a problem that cost them an application.
- Give specific, constructive feedback. Assume good faith.
- Keep personal information out of the project: never post anyone's real application data, names, addresses or saved pages.
- No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, sexualised language, or doxxing.

## Scope
Applies in the repository, issues, pull requests and any space where you represent the project.

## Enforcement
Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close threads, and block people who break these rules. Report problems privately
through GitHub's "Report content" or the contact in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports are handled confidentially.

This is a short, original statement in the spirit of widely used community codes such as the Contributor Covenant
(https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), which you may adopt in full instead.
