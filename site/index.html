Formhand

Job application autofill for Firefox. 100% local. No account. Never presses Submit.

Download Source on GitHub

Your data stays in your browser

  • No network code. No analytics, telemetry, accounts or servers. The developer receives and keeps no personal information.
  • No website access until you ask. No content scripts. It touches only the tab you are on, only after you press Alt+Z. The one extra: host access to four Greenhouse, Lever and Ashby job-board addresses, so an application form they embed in a company page is filled on the same key press.
  • Declares "no data collection" to Firefox, and says why each permission exists.
  • Yours to delete. One "Delete all my data" button in Preferences wipes every profile, answer and setting. Exports are plain-text files, and the options page says so next to the button.
  • Small and readable. Plain JavaScript, no dependencies at runtime, no build step, MIT licensed.

How it works

  1. Save a profile and the standard answers you are comfortable with. Leave any blank and it is never filled.
  2. Open an application and press Alt+Z (or use the toolbar button).
  3. Read the page and the summary panel: click a count to jump to the next field to check. Fix the orange and yellow fields. Press Next or Submit yourself.
What the outlines mean
Green, solidFilled from a confident match
Orange, dashedFilled, please review
Yellow, dottedA required field that needs you

It never

  • presses Next, Continue, Submit, Back, Cancel or links;
  • ticks consent, agreement or signature boxes;
  • fills salary expectations, emergency contacts or references;
  • overwrites something you already typed, or guesses a sensitive answer.

Works with

Dedicated handling for Phenom People, Greenhouse, Avature, Workday and Eightfold application pages, plus a generic filler for ordinary forms. Greenhouse, Lever and Ashby forms embedded in a company's career page are filled in place; other embedded application forms open in their own tab. Custom dropdowns on these platforms were tested against simulations, so please report anything that behaves differently. No dedicated adapters yet for Lever, Ashby, iCIMS or SmartRecruiters.
