Profiles

Keep one profile per variant, e.g. "Home address" and "Campus address". The profile you edit here is not necessarily the one used for filling.

Profile details

Standard answers

These questions repeat on most applications with different wording. They are recognised across wording variants and filled only when an option clearly matches. Blank means never filled.

Q&A bank

Learning starts on a page after you press Alt+Z there once (or use the toolbar Fill button); answers you then type/pick yourself are saved.

Company names in saved questions

Questions and answers are saved with {company} in place of the hiring company ("Have you ever worked at {company} before?"), so one entry covers every company and the answer is filled with the current company's name. Entries saved earlier may still name a company: convert them here.

    Export / import

    Import merges: matching profile names and questions are updated, new ones are added, and nothing already stored is ever deleted.

    The export file holds your personal data in plain text. Everything in your profiles, standard answers and Q&A bank (name, contact details, address, answers) is in it, unencrypted and readable by anyone or any program that can open the file. Keep it somewhere private and delete copies you no longer need.

    Delete all my data

    Removes every profile, your standard answers, the whole Q&A bank and all settings (learn mode, remembered company names) from this browser. Formhand then starts as after a fresh install: one empty profile, and only the four default "No" standard answers (previously employed, client, relatives, referred). This cannot be undone: export first if you may want the data back. Files you exported earlier are not touched.
